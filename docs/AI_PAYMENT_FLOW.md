# AI Payment Flow

1. The AI may prepare a checkout action but must return a pending action for explicit confirmation.
2. Checkout validates the customer address and sends server product identifiers, quantities and the selected method; client totals are not sent as authoritative values.
3. The backend recalculates price, voucher, stock and shipping, then creates the order.
4. The customer—not the AI—selects COD, MoMo or VNPAY.
5. For online payments the frontend calls the backend's order-specific payment endpoint and redirects only to its HTTP(S) payment URL.
6. Browser redirects never mark an order paid. The backend's signed MoMo/VNPAY IPN verifies signature and amount, atomically updates the order, and records duplicate-safe transactions.

QR is intentionally not generated by the frontend. The audited create endpoints return a hosted payment URL, not provider-authorized QR payloads. Therefore QR display is **BLOCKED** until the backend contract exposes verified QR data.
